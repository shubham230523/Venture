# 🚀 Venture

**Your startup. Your decisions. Your consequences.**

Venture is an **AI-powered startup simulation game** where you build and run your own company. Make decisions, manage your team, raise funding, compete with other companies, and see how your choices shape your startup's future.

## ✨ Features

* 🏢 Create and manage your startup
* 💰 Manage revenue, expenses, funding, valuation, and runway
* 👥 Hire and manage employees with unique personalities
* 🤖 AI Co-Founder and AI executive team
* 🧑‍💼 AI investors and funding negotiations
* 🏆 AI-powered competitors that react to your strategy
* 👨‍💻 AI customers and realistic feedback
* 📈 Product, marketing, and growth decisions
* ⚡ Random market events and business crises
* 🗣️ AI-powered conversations and negotiations
* 🎙️ Voice-enabled AI characters
* 🧠 AI Game Master that creates dynamic events and stories
* 📊 Post-game analysis of your founder strategy
* 💾 Save and continue your company journey

## 🧠 AI + Game Engine

Venture separates **AI from the core game simulation**.

The deterministic game engine controls things like money, revenue, employees, valuation, customers, and victory conditions.

AI handles conversations, personalities, events, recommendations, negotiations, and storytelling.

This makes the simulation predictable, testable, and scalable while still providing a dynamic experience.
