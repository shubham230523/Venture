# Board Room Experience

## Purpose
The Board Room is Venture's signature decision-making arena. At quarterly milestones, the player convenes with AI executives (CTO, CFO) and lead investors.

---

## Screen Flow & Player Experience
1. **Executive Debate**: CTO advocates technical quality and infrastructure investments; CFO argues for capital efficiency; Lead Investor demands growth milestones.
2. **Founder Decision Vote**: The player casts the tie-breaking board vote, shaping company strategy and executive relationships.
